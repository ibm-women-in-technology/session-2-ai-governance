# Cleaning up the environment

1. Go to the tab showing the **list of registered use cases**.
1. In the search bar, enter **your initials or other identifier** for your own use case.
1. Your use case is not active at the moment, since you completed the entire workflow and have not started a new one.
1. **Unselect** the **Active Only** checkbox to the right of the search bar. <img src="./assets/image_087.png">
1. Your use case should now be in the list
1. Tick the **selection box** to the left of your use case’s name, then select **Delete** from the toolbar. <img src="./assets/image_088.png">
1. This will automatically also delete all the associated objects such as assessments, risks and reviews.
1. Done!
